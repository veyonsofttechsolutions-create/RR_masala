# RR MASALA — International Food E-Commerce Build

This build adds single-INR product pricing, domestic/international order classification, AIR/SEA shipping selection, PhonePe/UPI payment adapter, stock release on cancellation/payment failure, legal policy center, grievance placeholders, and product-level food/export metadata.

Before production, the business advocate/CA/CS/export consultant must approve final policies, registrations, labels and destination-country requirements. Do not invent FSSAI/IEC/CRES/APEDA/HSN or grievance details. Configure PhonePe credentials only on the server.
